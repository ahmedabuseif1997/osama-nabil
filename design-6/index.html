About

Arabic & English. Print & digital.

My work spans the full life of a brand: the mark, the system built around it, and the everyday content that keeps it visible. I’ve designed for clinics, cafés, real-estate developers, car-rental and mobility brands, accounting firms, marketing agencies and large events — in both Arabic and English.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does Osama Nabil design?

Brand identities and guideline books, social media designs and campaigns, print and event collateral (brochures, passes, roll-ups, exhibition stands, signage and vehicle branding), company profiles and product catalogues, and Arabic typography.

Which countries has Osama Nabil worked with?

Brands in the United Arab Emirates, Saudi Arabia, Oman and Egypt — including clinics, cafés, real-estate developers, car-rental and mobility companies, accounting firms, marketing agencies and large events.

Can he design in both Arabic and English?

Yes. The portfolio includes bilingual brand guidelines and social media work typeset natively in Arabic and English.
